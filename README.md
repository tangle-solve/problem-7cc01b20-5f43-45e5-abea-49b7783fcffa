### CHEM-000286: C–B axial chirality that survives Lewis-base coordination
- **Subfield:** Round 2
- **Open:** Design axially chiral boron Lewis acids retaining configuration through repeated donor association and dissociation without sacrificing Lewis acidity.
- **Status:** Doan et al. showed in 2025 that Lewis-base binding can accelerate triarylborane atropisomerization from decades to minutes. Stability of the free borane therefore leaves catalytic-cycle stability unresolved.
- **Type:** experimental
- **Why:** Makes C–B atropisomers persistent asymmetric Lewis-acid catalysts.
- **Key works:** T.-H. Doan et al. (2025), *Atropisomerism in Triarylboranes: Lewis Base Assisted Rotation at C−B Stereogenic Axis in Asymmetrical Boron Lewis Acids*, [DOI:10.1002/ange.202421931](https://doi.org/10.1002/ange.202421931).
